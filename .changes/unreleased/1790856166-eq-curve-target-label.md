type: Changed
title: The EQ shown, the target said
---

The Record screen's EQ button and the player's show the EQ's curve, not only its name; the low cut is now a switch of its own that no starting point turns on or off (so the "No rumble" starting point is gone); the target level is written over the meter while listening and recording; and the app's mark in the top bar is no longer cut off at its edge.
