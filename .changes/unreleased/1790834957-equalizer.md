Shape how a recording sounds with a low cut and five knobs — Bass, Warmth, Mids, Presence and Air, each from off to +12 dB — or a starting point like Podcast or Warm, with the sound drawn under the curve showing what the EQ adds and takes away, heard in the player and baked into exports without ever changing the recording; set the EQ new takes start with on the Record screen and monitor the microphone through it on headphones.
